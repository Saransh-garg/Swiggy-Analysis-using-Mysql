# **Swiggy SQL Analysis**
## **Project Overview**
This project involves analyzing the Swiggy dataset to derive actionable insights using SQL queries. By exploring data on customer behaviour, restaurant performance, order trends, and delivery efficiency, the goal is to help Swiggy improve strategic decision-making in areas such as marketing, customer engagement, restaurant partnerships, and logistics optimization.

## **Objectives & Key Questions**
### **Customer Insights:**
- Identify customers based in specific cities like 'Delhi' or 'Mumbai.'
- Track customers who have placed at least one order and those who haven’t.
- Determine the total number of orders placed by each customer.
- Analyze the number of orders placed on specific days to understand customer frequency.
### **Restaurant Performance:**
- Calculate the total revenue generated by each restaurant.
- Find the average rating of all restaurants in a particular city (e.g., Mumbai).
- Identify the top 5 restaurants with the highest average rating.
### **Order Trends:**
- Analyze the total number of orders placed in the last 30 days.
- Track orders based on location (e.g., Mumbai) and time of day for demand insights.
- Identify restaurants with significant order volume and customer preference.
### **Delivery Partner Performance:**
- Identify delivery partners who have completed more than 1 delivery.
- Find the delivery partner who has worked with the most different customers.
- Explore delivery trends and identify partners working with a higher volume of customers.
## **SQL Queries for In-Depth Analysis**
The project uses various advanced SQL queries and joins to address the following:

- Filter and aggregate data based on customer location, activity, and order behavior.
- Perform calculations to track restaurant performance (revenue, ratings) and delivery partner activity.
- Analyze order trends by time, location, and customer frequency to uncover key patterns in Swiggy's operations.
## **Features**
- #### **Advanced SQL Queries:** Utilizes intricate joins, aggregation functions, and filtering to generate insights.
- #### **Performance-Optimized Queries:** Queries are structured to ensure efficient execution on large datasets.
- #### **Data-Driven Insights:** Focused on customer engagement, restaurant performance, and delivery efficiency.
## **Key Insights**
- #### **Customer Behavior:** Insights into customer activity help optimize marketing strategies and improve customer retention. Identifying inactive customers allows targeted re-engagement efforts.
- #### **Restaurant Performance:** Revenue and average ratings provide a clear picture of top-performing restaurants, which can guide Swiggy in strengthening partnerships with high-performing establishments.
- #### **Order Trends:** Tracking order volumes and patterns, especially during peak times or specific locations, helps in managing resources efficiently and anticipating demand spikes.
- #### **Delivery Efficiency:** Analyzing delivery partner performance helps optimize logistics, ensuring timely deliveries and better customer satisfaction.
## **Conclusion**
By leveraging SQL queries to analyze Swiggy's dataset, this project provides valuable insights into customer engagement, restaurant performance, and delivery efficiency. The findings can help Swiggy make data-driven decisions for improving customer experiences, streamlining operations, and enhancing restaurant partnerships. Regular updates and continuous analysis can further optimize business strategies and contribute to Swiggy’s growth and success.
